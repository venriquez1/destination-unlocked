Recommends U.S. destinations based on short-term National Weather Service (NWS) forecasts and your weather preferences.

Transparency & how scoring works

This app ranks destinations using available short-term NWS forecast periods. A period “matches” if:

  • Temperature is within your selected range, and
  • Probability of precipitation is at or below your maximum.

Destination Score (0–100) = (qualifying forecast periods ÷ total valid forecast periods) × 100.

A higher score means the forecast better matches your weather preferences — not that it’s the best overall vacation experience. Forecasts can change.

Data source: National Weather Service (api.weather.gov). Built for a class project prototype.
